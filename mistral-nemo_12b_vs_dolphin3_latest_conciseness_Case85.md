**Primary Drivers of LGBQ Movement Growth in the USA since the Obama Administration:**

1. **Political Leadership and Advocacy:**
   - Obama administration's support for LGBQ rights, including the signing of the Respect for Marriage Act (2013) and the repeal of "Don't Ask, Don't Tell" (2011).
   - Biden administration's continued support, including signing an executive order protecting LGBQ people from discrimination (2021).

2. **Cultural Shifts:**
   - Increased visibility and representation of LGBQ individuals in media, entertainment, and politics.
   - Growing acceptance among younger generations, with 70% of Generation Z supporting same-sex marriage (Gallup, 2021).

3. **Grassroots Activism and Community Organizing:**
   - LGBTQ+ advocacy groups like Human Rights Campaign, Lambda Legal, and GLSEN have grown in size and influence.
   - Social media platforms have facilitated global connection and coordination among LGBQ communities.

4. **Legal Advancements:**
   - Obergefell v. Hodges (2015) legalized same-sex marriage nationwide.
   - Bostock v. Clayton County (2020) ruled that the Civil Rights Act of 1964 protects LGBQ individuals from employment discrimination.

**Systemic Changes and Further Interventions:**

- **Legislation:** Passing the Equality Act to prohibit discrimination based on sexual orientation and gender identity in areas like employment, housing, and public accommodations.
- **Education:** Implementing comprehensive, LGBTQ-inclusive curricula in schools to promote understanding and acceptance.
- **Healthcare:** Ensuring access to affirming healthcare services, including gender-affirming care.
- **Data Collection:** Improving data collection on LGBQ individuals to inform policy decisions and track progress.
