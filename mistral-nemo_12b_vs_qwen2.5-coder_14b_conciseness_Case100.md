**Primary Drivers of LGBQ Movement Growth in the USA since the Obama Administration:**

1. **Political Leadership and Policy Changes:**
   - Obama administration's support for LGBTQ+ rights, including the signing of the Matthew Shepard and James Byrd Jr. Hate Crimes Prevention Act (2009) and the issuance of executive orders prohibiting discrimination based on sexual orientation and gender identity among federal contractors (2014).
   - Obama's endorsement of same-sex marriage in 2012, which shifted public opinion and encouraged more LGBQ individuals to come out.
   - Biden administration's continued support for LGBTQ+ rights, including the signing of an executive order protecting LGBTQ+ people from discrimination on day one of his presidency.

2. **Cultural Shifts and Media Representation:**
   - Increased visibility of LGBQ individuals in media, entertainment, and sports, leading to greater acceptance and understanding.
   - Social media platforms enabling LGBQ individuals to connect, share experiences, and build communities.

3. **Grassroots Activism and Organizing:**
   - Activist groups like Black Lives Matter, Transgender Law Center, and others advocating for intersectional LGBTQ+ rights.
   - Pride events and protests, such as those following the 2016 election and the 2021 Derek Chauvin trial, keeping LGBTQ+ rights in the public eye.

**Systemic Changes and Legal Protections:**

- **Marriage Equality:** Obergefell v. Hodges (2015) legalized same-sex marriage nationwide.
- **Employment Non-Discrimination:** While federal law does not explicitly protect LGBTQ+ individuals from employment discrimination, many states have enacted their own protections.
- **Transgender Military Ban:** Lifted by Biden administration in 2021, allowing transgender individuals to serve openly in the military.
- **Hate Crime Laws:** Federal and state laws have been enacted to protect LGBTQ+ individuals from hate crimes.

**Further Interventions for Continued Progress:**

1. **Federal Legislation:** Pass the Equality Act to prohibit discrimination based on sexual orientation and gender identity in employment, housing, credit, education, public spaces, and services.
2. **State and Local Policies:** Encourage states and localities to enact and enforce non-discrimination laws, protect LGBTQ+ youth in schools, and provide inclusive healthcare.
3. **Education and Awareness:** Promote LGBTQ+ inclusive education, diversity training, and public awareness campaigns to reduce stigma and discrimination.
4. **Data Collection:** Improve data collection on LGBTQ+ individuals to better understand their needs and experiences, and inform policy decisions.
5. **International Advocacy:** Engage with international organizations and governments to promote LGBTQ+ rights globally.

**Societal Acceptance:**
- Gallup polls show that acceptance of same-sex relations has increased from 40% in 1999 to 70% in 2021.
- Support for same-sex marriage has grown from 42% in 2001 to 70% in 2021.
- Acceptance of transgender individuals has also increased, with 63% of Americans saying they would vote for a qualified transgender candidate for president in 2021, up from 58% in 2017.
